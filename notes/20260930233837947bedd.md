To avoid conflating Material Up navigation with browser Back navigation, the URL bar was converted to a bottom bar with dedicated Back and Forward arrow icons. History entries store both Url and immutable PageState (HistoryEntry) in backStack/forwardStack, enabling instantaneous 0ms back/forward traversal for lightweight gemtext without network roundtrips. Reloading is triggered via Enter/Go and preserves existing history stacks. Android system back is unified across platforms via an expect/actual BackHandler delegating to androidx.activity.compose.BackHandler on Android and no-ops on desktop/web/iOS.
