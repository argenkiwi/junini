When checkServerTrusted throws (e.g. a TOFU mismatch), Ktor's cio-tls-closer coroutine fails with ClosedWriteChannelException in the context passed to Socket.tls(). That context has its own Job (see the hang gotcha) and no handler, so the exception is uncaught: it is only logged on Desktop but would crash the app on Android. GeminiTransport.jvm.kt therefore adds a no-op CoroutineExceptionHandler (IgnoreTlsSessionErrors) to the TLS context. The real error still reaches the caller through fetch(). This was found with a live test against geminiprotocol.net and does not show up with fake transports.
