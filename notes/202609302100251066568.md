Most Gemini capsules use self-signed certificates, and the spec recommends TOFU. Iteration 1 deliberately uses an AcceptAllTrustManager (GeminiTransport.jvm.kt), a choice the user confirmed during planning, to keep scope small. The next step is TOFU: pin the first certificate fingerprint per host (persisted) and warn on mismatch. Ktor calls checkServerTrusted with the chain, so pinning can live in that trust manager.
